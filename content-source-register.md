# Content source register

Internal register for the presentation-ready public website. This file is intentionally not linked from the visitor interface.

The following content supports the concept presentation and requires replacement, confirmation, or formal Faculty approval before public publication:

- Student, graduate, faculty and employer statistics.
- Program names, study duration, credit hours, curricula and career-path descriptions.
- Admission requirements, transfer rules, application dates, tuition and payment information.
- Faculty leadership names, biographies and academic titles.
- Accreditation, recognition and quality statements.
- Partner, employer and training-provider names.
- News stories, event dates and event registration details.
- Alumni names, roles, quotations and career outcomes.
- Contact email addresses, phone numbers, office hours, map location and social channels.
- All generated photography and any captions that suggest a particular place or event.

## Presentation safeguards

- The visitor interface does not display draft, placeholder, pending, demo or temporary labels.
- The showcase avoids named third-party partnerships, rankings and formal accreditation claims.
- Contact and application interactions behave locally for demonstration and do not send personal data.
